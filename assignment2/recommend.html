Transparent picks

How we choose your movies

1. Safety filters first

  • Child – only G and PG movies are suggested.
  • Teen – G, PG and M movies (no MA15+).
  • Adult – every classification.
  • Family viewing is limited to G and PG, as children are likely to be watching too.

2. Then every movie is scored

  • Your genre is the movie’s main genre: +5 points
  • Your genre is a secondary genre: +3 points
  • The movie suits your viewing occasion: +3 points
  • The movie was made for your age group: +2 points

The three highest-scoring movies are shown with a match percentage and the reasons they were chosen.
